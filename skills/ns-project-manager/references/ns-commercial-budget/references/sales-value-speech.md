# Sales value speech (client export)

**Locked brief.** Do not improvise structure, addressee, extra headings, or tone. Transform the scoped budget / descritivo into a **value-oriented commercial speech** for a sales executive to read **aloud to the client decision-maker** (CEO, CFO, operations director). Role: senior sales consultant for software and technology.

Section **Valor agregado desta versão** in `commercial-budget-costumer.md` — immediately after Objetivo. Only the four headings below. No Resumo executivo, no “Por que esta cotação”, no “Custo do não fazer”, no Feature dump.

## Addressee (fixed)

Speak **to the decision-maker**, about **organizational** result. The operator / analyst may be who *uses* the product; they are **not** who this section is written for.

Optional context (infer from brief; do not invent a persona interview): client segment, main reported pain, alternatives under evaluation.

## Input

The descritivo / commercial budget being written. Ground claims in scoped deliveries and stated exclusions. Do not invent capabilities. Do not recap the quote (“esta cotação paga…”, “já compraram o motor…”).

## Produce — four PT-BR headings, verbatim, in this order

### Headline de valor

One impact sentence. What the client **gains**, not what we deliver. Business result, not technology.

- Imperative or transformative (“Transforme X em Y”). Speakable in one breath.
- No product names as the hero, no Feature numbers, no stack.
- Fail: cost-recovery / quote framing (“the data you already paid for…”).

### Parágrafo de valor agregado

One complete paragraph, 120–180 words PT-BR, executive, formal, result-oriented. Written to be read aloud in a meeting. No bullets, no markers, no internal subheadings.

Must:

1. Open with the **new organizational capability**, not the interface and not the invoice.
2. Name the **central differentiator in business terms** (time, evidence, coverage, risk) — “the difference is not the screen; it is X that today takes days.”
3. Connect the **main scoped deliveries** to that result.
4. Close on **business protection** (rework avoided, investment protected, next cycles cheaper) when the brief says so.

Forbidden in this paragraph: architecture, frameworks, languages, unexplained technical acronyms, development jargon, raw feature lists, colloquial tone, “esta linha de cotação”, already-paid infrastructure as the story.

Write a **new** paragraph from **this** scope. Do not copy sample speeches.

### Ganhos concretos esperados

**3–5** items. Each is a measurable business gain, not a scoped volume restated as a trophy.

Format (adapt the metric, keep the skeleton):

`Redução de X% em [processo], liberando Y horas/mês para [função]`

- Prefer a **range** when the stakeholder gave no number (`entre 20% e 40%`, hours freed for a named role).
- If a real market benchmark exists, **cite it** in the same item (source + year).
- `[⚠️ validar com cliente]` only on the clause that is not in the brief (e.g. today’s audit volume). Do **not** stamp every line.
- Mix axes when the scope supports them: time, search quality, compliance/risk, coverage of an already-stated volume, amortization of later versions.
- Never a fabricated single-point R$ payback (`anti-hallucination.md`).

**Fail:** restating scope facts as the gain (volume, hop count, “100% of archive”).  
**Pass:** time cut + hours freed; search quality; compliance/risk; coverage only when tied to a business outcome; later versions reuse this foundation when the brief says so.

### Possíveis objeções + resposta sugerida

**2–3** pairs. Objections a **decision-maker** raises about **trusting and using** the solution. Rebut from **this version’s scoped behavior**. Tone: sure, direct, not defensive.

Each pair:

1. Quoted objection (the fear).
2. Short rebuttal: what the delivery **does** that answers the fear — still business language. If a mechanism must be named, explain it in plain Portuguese.

**Required kinds of objection (pick the ones the scope actually supports):**

| Kind | Example fear |
|------|----------------|
| Trust / error | “AI invents facts; unfit for this operation.” |
| Overlap | “We already have a similar tool in the current platform.” |
| Risk / permission | “How do we know it will not show what the user cannot see?” |

**Forbidden objections** (procurement / quote theater — never use):

- “Why another quote if we already have AI / already paid for the engine?”
- “Can we index a sample first?”
- “Why not wait for version 2?”
- Price, GPU, invoice sequencing.

## Output rules (non-negotiable)

- PT-BR, executive, formal, direct, result-anchored (time, money, risk, competitiveness).
- Four headings only, names unchanged.
- Vague fact: `[⚠️ validar com cliente]` — do not fabricate a precise figure.
- Do not let the model “choose a commercial angle” (cost already sunk, selling the quote, talking to the analyst). The angle is **always** organizational gain for the decision-maker, as above.
